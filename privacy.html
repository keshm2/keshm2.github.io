Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This is a personal portfolio site hosted on GitHub Pages. It is deliberately simple and collects as little as possible.

What this site stores

  • Theme preference. If you switch the light/dark theme, that choice is saved in your browser's localStorage on your device only. It is never sent anywhere and I cannot see it.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts.
  • No cookies.
  • No contact form. If you email me, that message lives in my mailbox and nowhere else on this site.
  • No third-party embeds that profile you.

Hosting logs

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data (such as IP address and user agent) for security and operational purposes under GitHub's privacy statement. I do not have access to those logs.

External links

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and project demos lead to sites with their own privacy policies. This policy covers only keshm2.github.io.

Contact

Questions about this policy: keshm@uw.edu. I reply within one business day.
